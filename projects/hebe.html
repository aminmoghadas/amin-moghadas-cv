Hebe

Visit hebe.gold →

Context

Hebe.gold is the sister brand to Talajat under Imaj Holding, with its own distinct identity in the online gold retail space. Where Talajat was a rebuild on top of an existing site, Hebe was a clean greenfield project — designed and shipped from scratch on WordPress and Elementor, with custom code modules layered on top for the business-specific features that the standard WooCommerce stack couldn't deliver cleanly.

It shares the unified accounting and inventory infrastructure built for Talajat, so stock, pricing, and sales stay in sync across both storefronts and the external marketplaces both brands sell on — no manual reconciliation required.
