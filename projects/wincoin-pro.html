Context

Wincoin.pro is the company's marketing website for their enterprise customer-loyalty offering — separate from the consumer SaaS platform. I designed and built the site end-to-end on WordPress and Elementor: brand-led visual design, content structure, and the Elementor templates that the team can maintain without developer involvement.
